Frequently asked

Is HoppingSite really free?

Yes. Publishing runs entirely on free GitHub Pages hosting, with no hidden fees.

Can I upload more than one file?

Not yet - HoppingSite accepts one self-contained index.html file per site.

What happens when a site expires?

Its address is released for anyone to claim - choose "Never" to keep it permanently.
